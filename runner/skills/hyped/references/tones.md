# Tones

The job prompt may set a tone (a preset or free words such as "calm and premium"). Free words refine or override the
preset; with no tone, pick the preset that suits the product and the scenario. The tone changes pacing, type, motion,
transitions and sound, never the craft rules.

| Tone | Feel | Scenes in 20 s | Type | Motion and transitions | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `default` | Punchy, playful, clean | 4-5 | Bold sans, tight | Brisk entrances, soft pushes and wipes | Upbeat track, a few light effects |
| `polished` | Serious, elegant, restrained | 3-4 | Lighter weights, more space | Slow eases, long holds, soft fades and dips | Calm track, very few effects |
| `cinematic` | Trailer scale, big claims | 4-5 | Very large type, wide tracking for small lines | Dramatic wipes and zooms, slow camera | Builds and lands on strong cues, a riser into the reveal |
| `app-store` | Clean feature cards | 4-6 | Product's own UI type, neat labels | Smooth slides; device frames, except in store previews (hyped-app: the stores reject them) | Light track, soft clicks |
| `chaotic` | Fast, loud, high energy | 6-8, some under 2 s | Heavy caps, big | Hard cuts, flashes, punch-ins | Fast track, more hits |
| `deadpan` | Calm, dry, nothing is a joke | 3-4 | Plain, small, lots of empty space | Slow fades, still frames that still breathe | Sparse or none |
| `yc-parody` | A startup launch played straight | 4-5, one claim each | Corporate sans, earnest | Hard cuts on each claim | Earnest corporate track |

Rules that hold in every tone:

- The hook still lands by second 3 and the call to action still ends the video.
- Text stays on screen long enough to read, even in `chaotic`: speed comes from cuts and motion, not from pulling
  text away early.
- Humour comes from the product's own absurdity, never from jokes you add.
