# Platform packs

Where a Hyped video gets posted, and what each place needs. The job prompt fixes the format and the length; when the
brief, the template or the notes name a place, use its row here for the hook, the text, the pacing inside the length,
the end card and the share copy. If the job's format isn't the one a place prefers, make the best of the job's format
(the customer chose it) and don't mention it.

Platform specs change often. Numbers marked "as of Oct 2026" were checked then; a newer number in the brief wins.

## At a glance

| Place | Best format | Length | Sound | Hook by | End card |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | vertical | 15–45 s | on for most viewers | 1 s | name and logo; "link in bio" or the words to search |
| Instagram Reels | vertical | 15–45 s | many start muted | 1 s | name and logo; "link in bio" |
| Instagram and Facebook feed | portrait (square works) | 15–45 s | muted | 1 s | URL, logo, name |
| Facebook and Instagram ads | portrait for feeds, plus a vertical cut for Reels and Stories | 6–15 s (30 s at most) | muted in feeds, often on in Reels | 1 s; product by 1.5 s | the offer and the same action as the ad's button |
| TikTok ads | vertical | 9–20 s | on | 1 s; product by 2 s | the offer and the action, above the button |
| YouTube Shorts | vertical | 15–45 s | on more often than not | 1 s | name; the words to search |
| YouTube | landscape | 30–90 s | on | 3 s | URL, logo, name, held 2.5–3 s |
| LinkedIn | portrait or square; landscape for desktop-heavy B2B | 30–60 s | muted | 1–2 s | URL, logo, name |
| X | square or portrait; landscape for developer audiences | 15–45 s | muted | 1 s | URL or handle |
| Product Hunt | landscape | 30–60 s (90 s at most) | on (it's clicked) | 3 s | name, logo, URL |
| App Store and Google Play | the store's own frame | 15–30 s | muted | 1 s | the app's own final screen |
| Homepage hero | landscape, or the hero box's own shape | loop 10–30 s; a demo behind a click 60–90 s | none for the loop; on for the demo | frame 0 | none for a loop; URL or sign-up for the demo |
| GitHub README | landscape | 15–45 s | none | 2 s | the install command and the repo name |

## What covers the frame, text rules, file limits

| Place | Over the video | Captions and text | File limits (as of Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | buttons down the right, caption and name along the bottom, tabs across the top: the vertical guide's box (x 120–960, y 270–1250) clears them | Hyped's burned-in captions; turn TikTok's auto-captions off so they don't double | in-app uploads up to 10 min, 60 min from a computer |
| Instagram Reels | top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6% (Meta's Reels zones): the vertical box | Hyped's captions; leave Instagram's own captions off | Reels up to 3 min; the profile grid shows the centre 3:4 (y 240–1680) |
| Instagram and Facebook feed | a sound button and a tag icon on the bottom corners: the portrait guide's box | text carries every point; headlines of 5 words or fewer | — |
| Facebook and Instagram ads | feeds: nothing over the picture; the ad's text sits above it, its headline and button below. Reels and Stories: Meta's 14% / 35% / 6% zones | keep the video's text to the hook, labels and the end card; don't repeat the ad's text fields word for word | up to 4 GB; Meta accepts 1080×1350 (its guides suggest up to 1440×1800 for 4:5) |
| TikTok ads | the ad caption (up to 100 characters), display name and button sit over the bottom left; buttons down the right. TikTok's safe-zone overlay shrinks as the ad caption grows, so keep everything in the vertical box | Hyped's captions; the ad caption is a separate field | ≤ 500 MB; TopView 5–60 s (TikTok suggests 9–15 s) |
| YouTube Shorts | title, channel and buttons: about the top 10%, bottom 25% and right 10%; the vertical box clears them | Hyped's captions | up to 3 min |
| YouTube | the title bar and controls on hover or pause (top 8%, bottom 10%); the length badge on the thumbnail's bottom right; end-screen cards over the last 5–20 s if the uploader adds them | upload `captions.srt` as a subtitle track; burned-in captions are optional here | a custom thumbnail of 1280×720 or more: use `brag.jpg` |
| LinkedIn | sound and captions buttons on the bottom right | burned-in captions, or upload `captions.srt` to LinkedIn instead: never both | organic 3 s–15 min, ≤ 5 GB; video ads ≤ 200 MB |
| X | the time badge bottom left, sound bottom right | burned-in captions | 2 min 20 s and 512 MB on free accounts; far longer with Premium |
| Product Hunt | YouTube's player (Product Hunt takes a YouTube link, not a file) | must still read muted: people often watch the gallery silently first | a public or unlisted YouTube video |
| App Store and Google Play | nothing; the store draws no device frame | short labels only; App Store previews must show footage captured from the app | App Store: 15–30 s, up to 3 per device size and language, 886×1920 (or 1920×886) for current iPhones, 1200×1600 for iPad, ≤ 500 MB, ≤ 30 fps. Google Play: a YouTube link |
| Homepage hero | the page's own headline and buttons beside or over it | no captions on a loop; a few large labels at most | aim for 2–6 MB for the loop |
| GitHub README | GitHub's player controls; it doesn't autoplay | the command and its output must read in the README column (~880 px wide on a desktop): text ≥ 40 px in a 1920 frame | ≤ 10 MB on free plans (100 MB on paid), .mp4, .mov or .webm |

## Notes

- **TikTok**: sound-on, fast and native. Open on the result or the problem in the first second; cut on the beat; a
  visible change every 1.5 s. URLs in captions aren't links, so the end card says the product's name (and "link in
  bio" if the brief says the profile has one).
- **Instagram Reels**: many start muted, so the hook is on screen, not only said. It loops: end on a calm frame that
  sits well before frame 0. Keep the poster's hook inside the 3:4 grid crop.
- **Instagram and Facebook feed**: a video post on Instagram is a Reel, but in the feed it's shown at its own shape, so
  4:5 fills the most screen. In the Reels tab (as of Oct 2026) a 4:5 video sits inside the 9:16 screen with the
  caption over its lower part: keep the hook and the end card above y 965 (the portrait guide).
- **Facebook and Instagram ads**: make the 4:5 cut for feeds and, when the customer runs Reels and Stories too, a
  9:16 cut (a second Hyped video in vertical). The first second must work as a thumbnail; the brand appears early
  (by 3 s) but small, never as an opening logo. The end card names the same action as the button ("Start free" over a
  "Sign up" button) and shows the URL or the offer.
- **TikTok ads**: like organic TikTok, but every second counts: the product in use by 2 s, one benefit, one ask. The
  ad caption, name and button cover the bottom left, so the end card's ask sits above y 1250.
- **YouTube Shorts**: like Reels; links in Shorts descriptions aren't clickable, so say the name to search. Shorts
  loop, so the last frame should lead back into the first.
- **YouTube**: viewers chose to watch, so a fuller story fits (the landscape guide's pacing). The thumbnail isn't
  frame 0 there: upload `brag.jpg` as the custom thumbnail. With an SRT track uploaded, burned-in captions double up
  for anyone who turns YouTube's on; the customer can turn Hyped's off in the editor.
- **LinkedIn**: watched at work, muted, on a phone more often than a desktop. Lead with the outcome for the viewer's
  job, not the feature list. The link goes in the post text; the end card repeats it.
- **X**: short and specific; the first frame does the work because the timeline autoplays muted. A post holds 280
  characters and a link counts as 23, so the share copy stays well under that.
- **Product Hunt**: put the launch video (landscape, 30–60 s) first in the gallery, as a YouTube link: it's what most
  visitors see first. Show the product working by second 3; end on the name and URL. Only say "live on Product
  Hunt" if the brief says it is.
- **App Store and Google Play**: app previews have their own sizes and rules (above); the `hyped-app` skill covers
  them. A Hyped vertical video isn't an App Store preview as it is: the frame differs (886×1920) and Apple wants only
  footage captured from the app.
- **Homepage hero**: autoplay works only muted (`<video autoplay muted loop playsinline poster="brag.jpg">`), so the
  loop tells the story with pictures and a few words, and its last frame matches its first. No end card on a loop:
  the page's own button is the call to action. A longer demo with a voiceover goes behind a "Watch the demo" click.
- **GitHub README**: drop the MP4 into the README in GitHub's web editor (it becomes a `user-attachments` link); it
  plays only when clicked, so the poster frame matters. Developers watch it muted: the real command, the real output,
  big enough to read. If the file is over 10 MB, shorten it or re-encode it at 1280×720.
